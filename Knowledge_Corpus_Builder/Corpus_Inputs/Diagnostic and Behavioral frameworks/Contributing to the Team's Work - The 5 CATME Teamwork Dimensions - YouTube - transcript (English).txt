[00:00] This video will explain the concept of contributing to the team's work one of the five CATME teamwork dimensions, Contributing to the Team's Work is defined as helping the team achieve its goals or objectives by completing the assigned tasks. This dimension is measured by the quality and quantity of a team members contributions to their team, their level of commitment and contribution to the team in its tasks, and whether or not they are helping their team members.To excel in this dimension, you must do more or higher
[00:29] quality work than expected and make important contributions that improve the team's work. This can be done by completing work accurately and on time, preparing for and participating in meetings, and keeping commitments even when it's difficult. Helping team members who are having difficulty completing their work is also very important to excel in this dimension.
[00:49] You can achieve this by teaching team members who are struggling to understand tasks how to complete them or volunteering your time to assist those who are falling behind in the work assigned to them.
