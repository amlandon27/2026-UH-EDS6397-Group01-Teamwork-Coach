[00:00] This video will explain the concept of Expecting Quality, one of the Five CATME Teamwork Dimensions. Expecting Quality focuses on the belief that the team is capable of and encouraging the team to strive for quality work. Excelling in this dimension requires the team members set high expectations and motivate their team to do excellent work.
[00:22] To set high expectations, you have to identify and share the benefits of the project. Expressing your belief that the team can do a good job builds confidence that can help sustain the team's efforts. Remember to allow each team member some level of control and responsibility for the project.
[00:39] Teams that do not care about the quality of their work are not going to perform well. Without a successful outlook team members may not follow through on their commitments. Teams are at their best when striving for and attaining challenging and realistic goals. Consider what expectations have benefited you and your teammates in the past. Thank you for watching!
