[00:00] This video will explain the concept of Having Relevant Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities, one of the Five CATME Teamwork Dimensions. Having relevant knowledge, skills, and abilities, or KSAs, is defined as possessing necessary competencies to help the team achieve their goals. KSAs are having and understanding information, knowing how to perform your tasks, and possessing the capabilities you need to complete the team's work.
[00:30] This dimension is measured by the quality of KSAs used in a project, the new skills or knowledge the team member is willing to learn, and their ability to perform the tasks of other team members. To excel in this dimension, a team member must demonstrate the KSAs to do excellent work, acquire new knowledge or skills to improve the team's performance, and be able to perform the role of any team member, if necessary.
[00:53] This can be accomplished by studying and practicing the relevant material, doing further research to help complete tasks, learning from experts in related fields, and challenging yourself to improve and learn more.
