[00:00] This video will explain the concept of Interacting with Teammates, one of the Five CATME Teamwork Dimensions. Interacting with Teammates is defined as providing positive interactions within the team that contribute to a supportive environment. This dimension is measured by the amount the team member listens to and respects their other team members and their ideas, their communication with their team, their participation and attitude during team activities, and how they respond to feedback from other team members.
[00:29] To excel in this dimension, you need to behave appropriately in meetings. This can be done through active listening or showing that you're paying attention and are fully engaged, communicating the proper information clearly, and being silent to allow others to talk and think. Creating a safe environment for team members is also very important.
[00:47] This can be done through providing encouragement or enthusiasm to your team, welcoming and respecting advice and feedback, asking team members for contributions and ideas, and making sure both you and your team members are understood.
