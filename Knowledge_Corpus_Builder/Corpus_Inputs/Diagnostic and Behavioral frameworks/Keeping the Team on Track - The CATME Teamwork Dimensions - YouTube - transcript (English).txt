[00:00] This video will explain the concept of Keeping the Team on Track, one of the Five CATME Teamwork Dimensions. Keeping the Team on Track involves efforts to progress towards achieving the team's goals. This includes observing conditions affecting the team and addressing them before they become a problem. Excelling in this dimension requires that team members watch conditions affecting the team and monitor the team's progress.
[00:23] This includes external conditions such as time, access to necessary resources, and changing client expectations. Don't forget to make sure each individual teammate is making appropriate progress. To excel in this dimension it is not enough to notice that things aren't going well. You need to give specific, timely, and constructive feedback.
